CARE THAT COMES TO YOU

Support Designed Around the Client and Family

Every person's care needs are different. Juels Health Care works with clients, families and healthcare professionals to understand the type of assistance needed at home.

Our Personal Support Workers can help with everyday personal care, mobility, companionship, meal support, routines and other non-medical activities included in the client's care plan.

Care can be arranged for short-term needs, recovery after hospitalization, respite for family caregivers, overnight support or ongoing assistance.

HOSPITAL-TO-HOME SUPPORT

Support After Hospital Discharge or Rehabilitation

Coming home from hospital can sometimes create new challenges for both the client and family.

Juels Health Care can work with clients, caregivers, discharge planners and other healthcare professionals to discuss the personal support required after discharge.

Depending on the care plan, support may include personal care, mobility assistance, meal support, medication reminders, companionship, overnight support and respite for family caregivers.

Planning a Discharge?

Contact Juels Health Care to discuss the client's home support needs, location, schedule and desired start date.
