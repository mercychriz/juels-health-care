JUELS HEALTH CARE

Supporting People at Home and Healthcare Organizations in the Community

Juels Health Care is committed to providing compassionate and professional support where it is needed most.

For individuals and families, we provide Personal Support Worker care in the comfort of home based on the client's individual needs, routines and care goals.

We can provide support after hospitalization or rehabilitation, assistance with personal care, respite for family caregivers, overnight support and ongoing home care.

We also partner with healthcare organizations requiring dependable workers for temporary coverage, scheduled shifts, vacancies, contracts and ongoing staffing needs.

PRIVATE HOME CARE SERVICES

Personalized PSW Support in the Comfort of Home

Our home-care services are designed to help clients remain safe, comfortable and supported while providing families with greater peace of mind.

01

Post-Hospital & Rehabilitation Support

Assistance after hospital discharge, surgery or rehabilitation to help support a safer transition home.

02

Personal Care

Respectful assistance with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting and activities of daily living.

03

Senior Home Care

Personalized assistance to help seniors remain comfortable, supported and independent in their homes.

04

Respite Care

Temporary support that gives family caregivers time to rest, work, attend appointments or manage other responsibilities.

05

Overnight Care

Overnight supervision and personal assistance for clients who may need support throughout the night.

06

24-Hour Care

Coordinated around-the-clock support for clients requiring continuous assistance and supervision.

07

Companionship & Daily Living Support

Friendly companionship and assistance with daily routines, meals and light household activities.

08

Mobility & Transfer Assistance

Support with walking, repositioning and transfers according to the client's established care plan.

09

Meal Preparation & Nutrition Support

Assistance with meal preparation, eating routines and hydration based on the client's individual needs.

10

Medication Reminders

Medication reminders and appropriate assistance within the Personal Support Worker's role and client's care plan.

11

Dementia & Memory Support

Compassionate assistance with routines, personal care, companionship and day-to-day support for clients experiencing memory changes.

12

Palliative & Family Support

Non-medical personal support and comfort-focused assistance alongside the client's family and healthcare team.
