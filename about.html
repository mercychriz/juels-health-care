ABOUT JUELS HEALTH CARE

Compassionate Care. Dependable Healthcare Support.

Juels Health Care supports individuals and families with private Personal Support Worker care at home while also helping healthcare organizations meet their staffing needs across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

WHO WE ARE

Supporting people at home and healthcare organizations in the community

Juels Health Care is a healthcare service provider focused on delivering compassionate Personal Support Worker care to individuals and families while also providing dependable healthcare staffing solutions to organizations.

For private clients, our goal is to help people remain safe, comfortable and supported in their homes through respectful assistance with personal care, daily living, companionship, post-hospital recovery, respite, overnight and ongoing care.

For healthcare organizations, we provide flexible workforce support designed to help manage staff shortages, employee absences, vacancies, scheduling gaps and increased service demands.

Whether we are supporting one person in their home or helping an organization maintain continuity of care, our focus remains professionalism, compassion, dignity and dependable service.
