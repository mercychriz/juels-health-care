ABOUT JUELS HEALTH CARE

A Healthcare Staffing Partner You Can Depend On

Juels Health Care provides flexible healthcare staffing solutions designed to help organizations maintain reliable care and dependable workforce coverage across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

WHO WE ARE

Supporting healthcare organizations with dependable staffing

Juels Health Care is a healthcare staffing company focused on connecting organizations with qualified Personal Support Workers and other healthcare professionals.

We understand that healthcare organizations can experience staffing shortages caused by employee absences, vacations, increased resident needs, vacancies and changing service demands.

Our goal is to provide flexible staffing solutions that help organizations maintain continuity of care while supporting the people they serve.
