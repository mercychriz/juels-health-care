FLEXIBLE STAFFING SUPPORT

Different Organizations. Different Staffing Needs.

Healthcare organizations have different workforce requirements. Hospitals may need additional healthcare workers for staffing shortages, while retirement residences and long-term care homes may require Personal Support Workers to assist residents with daily living and personal care.

Juels Health Care works with organizations to understand their staffing requirements and provide workforce support based on assignment needs, worker qualifications and staff availability.
