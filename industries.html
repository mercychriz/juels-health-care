FLEXIBLE STAFFING SUPPORT

Different Organizations. Different Staffing Needs.

Healthcare organizations have different workforce requirements. Hospitals may require additional healthcare workers during staffing shortages, while retirement residences and long-term care homes may require Personal Support Workers to assist residents and support continuity of care.

Juels Health Care works with organizations to understand their staffing requirements and discuss workforce support based on assignment needs, required qualifications, scheduling requirements and staff availability.
