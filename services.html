PERSONAL CARE & DAILY LIVING SUPPORT

Compassionate Support With Everyday Care Needs

Our Personal Support Workers assist clients with essential daily living activities while promoting dignity, comfort, independence and respect.

01

Daily Living Support

Assistance with everyday routines and activities based on each client's individual care and support needs.

02

Bathing & Personal Hygiene

Respectful assistance with bathing, showering and personal hygiene while maintaining privacy and dignity.

03

Grooming & Dressing

Assistance with grooming, dressing, hair care and maintaining personal appearance.

04

Eating & Feeding Assistance

Respectful mealtime assistance for clients who require additional support while eating.

05

Meal Preparation
